Security & Management Sign-Off Brief

Guidewire AI Query Studio

To: Engineering Manager & Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
Domain: Guidewire PolicyCenter, BillingCenter, ClaimCenter
Date: September 2026

1. Executive Summary

This project creates an internal, natural-language query assistant for insurance business users across Residential and Commercial product lines. Team members can ask everyday questions (e.g. "What is our loss ratio for Residential vs Commercial?" or "Show top 10 open Commercial claims with reserves over $50,000") and get instant PostgreSQL queries and charts without waiting on database administrator tickets.

🔒

100% Local & Air-Gapped (Zero Cloud Data Egress)

Uses local Ollama (127.0.0.1). Absolutely no prompts, database schemas, or company records are ever sent to OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, or any third-party cloud.

2. Key Security & Privacy Safeguards

1. Zero Policyholder Data to AI

The AI only receives structural table and column names (e.g. policynumber, lossdate). Zero customer records are ever fed to the AI model.

2. Automatic NPI / PII Masking

Dynamic Data Masking (DDM) automatically redacts sensitive data before display: SSN (***-**-1234), Bank Accounts, Emails, and Phone Numbers.

3. Read-Only Protection

Guaranteed read-only. DROP, DELETE, UPDATE, and INSERT are blocked by AST inspection and PostgreSQL READ ONLY transactions.

4. Anti-Hallucination Guardrails

AI cannot invent tables. If a question asks for non-existent information, the system safely returns: "Insufficient information to determine."

3. Zero-Risk Proof of Concept (POC)

For the initial evaluation, the application uses a 100% preloaded synthetic Guidewire demo database (fake customer names, fake claims, fake invoices). No real policyholder data will be accessed until Management and InfoSec review the working tool and inspect audit logs.
